A Note from Our Founder

We believe emotional intelligence is one of the most important skills a person can develop—yet one of the least taught.

The ability to understand ourselves, navigate emotions, build meaningful relationships, and respond thoughtfully to life's challenges shapes every aspect of how we live. These are not optional skills. They are foundational human skills.

Sthirabhava was founded with a simple conviction: emotional intelligence should be accessible to everyone, not reserved for moments of crisis or a privileged few.

We're building an ecosystem where science, technology, and human understanding come together to make emotional intelligence practical, accessible, and part of everyday life.

This is a long-term mission. We are not here to build another app—we are here to help build a generation that understands itself better, connects more deeply with others, and leads with greater awareness.

If our work helps even one person respond with more empathy, make one wiser decision, or better understand their own emotions, we are moving in the right direction.

Thank you for being part of this journey.

Pallavi Nagasamudram
Pallavi Nagasamudram
